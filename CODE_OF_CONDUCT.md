# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be kind. Discuss the work, not the person. Assume the other reader knows
things you do not.

Not welcome: personal attacks, harassment of any kind, publishing others' private information, and
sustained disruption of discussion.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement about a design is not a conduct problem, it is the point of review. State what you think
is wrong and why, and prefer evidence over assertion: a failing case, a measurement, a citation. If a
claim in this repository is wrong, showing that it is wrong is a contribution.

## Scope

Applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing this
project.

## Enforcement

Report to fsantibanez@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit, remove or
reject contributions and comments that are not aligned with this document, and may block accounts for
repeated or severe violations.
